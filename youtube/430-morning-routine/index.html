intro#

Staying Late at Night Is Usually Unproductive and unhealthy for me, so i decide to wake up early in the morning.

4:30 - 5:00 prepare appearance#

After I wake up, I brush my teeth, drink a glass of water,wash my face, wash my hair, and do my skincare routine. I like to dry my hair upside down — it helps my hair stand up and creates the illusion of having more volume.becasue When you flip your head over, the roots lift as they dry, which gives a fuller look. Before going out, I apply sunscreen, followed by a little bit of powder foundation and blush.

Cleaning myself in the morning not only helps me feel refreshed, but also prepares me emotionally for the day. When I step out of my apartment, I want to feel clear-headed, clean, and well-groomed.

I used to go to the gym right after waking up, brushing only my teeth beforehand. But I didn’t feel as confident as I do when I’m fully groomed.

Appearance does affect my confidence level. When I feel clean, fresh, and well-groomed, I naturally carry myself with more ease and confidence. It’s not about looking perfect — it’s about feeling put-together.

For example, when my hair is washed, my skin feels clean, and I’ve done a simple skincare and makeup routine, I feel more ready to face the world. It’s like I’m mentally prepared to handle challenges in life.

when I don’t take time for basic grooming, I tend to shrink back a little bit. to make it worse, i use a hat and a mask to hide myself. I avoid eye contact or feel a bit insecure and don’t have any confidence.

So, appearance doesn’t define my worth, but it definitely influences how i live my day.

Good grooming is important because it goes far beyond just looking neat. it directly impacts my self-confidence, social interactions, and success in life.

so i take half an hour in the morning to take care of my appearence. and i am well prepared to take chanlleange every day.

Preparing my appearance is part of how I mentally prepare for the day.

how to get up early#

  1. enjoy it The key to getting up early—and making it a sustainable habit—is to genuinely enjoy the experience. When waking up early feels rewarding rather than a chore, it becomes much easier to stick with it. as a mum, early moring is my own free time.

Why Enjoyment Is the Key Intrinsic Motivation: When you look forward to something in the morning, you’re naturally more motivated to get up. Enjoyment turns discipline into desire.

Positive Associations: If mornings are filled with activities you love, your brain starts to associate waking up early with pleasure rather than deprivation.

Sustainable Habit: Habits built on joy and meaning are much more likely to last than those based on willpower alone.

  1. expose to early morning sunshine

3.Start Small and Celebrate Wins

Wake up just 10–15 minutes earlier at first.

Celebrate each morning you get up early and enjoy it.

  1. use morning time to take care of yourself Establishing a morning routine focused on nurturing your physical, mental, and emotional well-being helps reduce stress, increase focus, and boost overall productivity. Sets Intentions: Starting your day with mindfulness or meditation helps clear your mind and set purposeful intentions for the day ahead.

Builds Healthy Habits: Incorporating self-care activities like hydration, stretching, and nourishing breakfast strengthens your body and mind.

Enhances Productivity: A calm, unrushed morning routine helps you feel in control and less stressed, which carries through your day.

Improves Mental Clarity: Avoiding distractions like early phone use and focusing on mindful practices supports emotional regulation and mental clarity.

Boosts Energy: Physical activities such as gentle stretching, yoga, or a morning walk increase blood flow and energize your body

Getting up early to take care of yourself is about creating a mindful, nurturing morning routine that supports your physical health, mental clarity, and emotional balance. This intentional start helps you feel energized, focused, and ready to face the day with confidence and calm.
