# **Deal Memo: Connection with Him**

## **Asset Details**
- **Asset:** Connection with Him  
- **Entry Date:** May 2025  
- **Holding Period:** ~2.5 months  
- **Total Interaction Time:** < 15 hours  
- **Capital Outlay:** $0 financial cost, high emotional capital  
- **Initial Intent:** Curiosity, openness, mutual encouragement  

---

## **Gains**

1. **Motivation Engine**  
   Harnessed relationship energy as fuel for **fitness**, **self-improvement**, **creativity**, and **emotional capital investing**.  

2. **Emotional Alchemy**  
   Converted sadness into love within days, proving emotional energy is transformable and under control.  

3. **Identity Expansion**  
   Broke parts of “habit of being yourself” that depended on others for validation.  

4. **Energy Awareness**  
   Recognized magnetic presence and began aligning with higher frequencies.  

5. **Emotional Sovereignty Training**  
   Learned detachment without shutting down love; kept heart open with boundaries intact.  

6. **Creative Boost**  
   Increased writing output, deep reflection, and self-worth insights.  

7. **Self-Generated Warmth**  
   Developed sustainable ability to feel love without external input.  

8. **Fitness Motivation**  
   Set tangible target: squat **92kg** within 2 years — physical strength mirrors emotional strength.  

9. **Letting Go Practice**  
   Began no-contact trial to systematically reduce emotional reliance.  

10. **Role Model Effect**  
    Used another person as a mirror to accelerate growth and self-awareness.  

---

## **Risk Management Actions**
- **Limited Exposure:** Short holding period, low total time invested.  
- **No Over-Allocation:** Majority of emotional capital remained self-focused.  
- **Strategic Exit:** No-contact plan to preserve gains and reduce volatility.  

---

## **Ongoing Dividends**
- Warmth in memory, reframeable at will.  
- Creative ideas sparked from small interactions.  
- Strengthened discernment in emotional investments.  
- Lifelong skill of self-fueling emotional states.  

---

## **Conclusion**
One of the **highest-return, lowest-risk trades** of my life.  
It cost almost nothing in time or resources but delivered tools, confidence, and energy that will **compound for decades**.  
The position is now closed for active trading — but the dividends are permanent.  

---

## **Mistakes to Avoid in Future**
- Emotional leverage was very high — like putting a small amount into a high-volatility stock.  
- Dollar/time input was small, but leverage amplified emotional portfolio swings.  
- The rapid cycle (**sadness → love → longing → independence**) was intense but became accelerated training in emotional risk management.  

---

## **Allocation Rules for Future**
- **80%** — Self (health, purpose, creativity, spiritual growth, friendships, investing)  
- **15–20%** — Romantic relationship (shared time, emotional presence)  
- **0–5%** — Unexpected spikes (situational emotions)  

---
## Ending

In less than three months, I experienced a sense of love and a surge of energy, creativity, and self-knowledge that might have taken years to develop otherwise.
Now, the chapter closes not with regret, but with gratitude.
Like a rare market event that leaves a lasting mark on an investor’s skill, this connection shaped me — and now it belongs to my history, not my future.
The dividends remain, and I walk forward lighter, stronger, and more focused on my core asset — myself.
