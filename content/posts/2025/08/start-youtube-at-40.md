This is the script for my very first YouTube video. I’m posting it here on my blog as a record of my clarity, so I can always look back and remember where I started.

“If you’re watching this in the future, maybe you’re wondering:
Why did she succeed?

The answer is simple: clarity.

I’m not here to try YouTube — I’ve committed for three years, 40 hours per week, no matter what.
I’ve already proven to myself that I can invest, write 300 blog posts, transform my body, and build strong daily habits.

Now, I’m bringing it all here — to inspire others through holistic wellbeing.

Clarity is the foundation of success.
And this is just the beginning.”

# Clarity
With clarity:
– The more clarity you have, the sharper your focus becomes.
– You know exactly what you want and why — and that creates strong motivation.
– Setbacks don’t throw you off, because you’re anchored in your purpose.
– When the ‘why’ is clear, the ‘how’ becomes lighter. Each video is just one more step, not a burden.
– You don’t chase trends or copy others blindly. You create from your core values.
– You have the strength to say no to distractions that don’t serve your goals.

## 1. Commitment
“I’m a stay-at-home mom, but I love to learn.
Over the last 10 years, I taught myself how to invest — and became a confident investor.
I studied computer science and once wrote over a thousand lines of code for an assignment.
Since 2018, I’ve written more than 300 blog posts.
And this year, when I decided to change my body, I did.

So I’m not here to try YouTube — I’m here to commit.
Three years, sharply focus on youtube, no matter what happens.
“The past won’t bother me — I’ve cleared my negative emotions and replaced them with love, trust, and gratitude.
The future won’t bother me — I’m patient, because I know the power of compounding.

I don’t care about short-term gains.
I care about long-term leverage.”

## 2. My Value
“I pursue holistic wellbeing in every aspect of life.

Physically, I can squat one and a half times my body weight while maintaining 20% body fat.
Financially, I’ve achieved over 40% year-to-date returns.
Emotionally, I’ve healed childhood trauma and broken negative family patterns.
Spiritually, I practice surrender and notice meaningful synchronicities.
Mentally, I think in systems — I’ve even built my own holistic wellbeing framework.

And I live this daily:
I wake up at 5 a.m., walk 15,000 steps, train with weights four times a week, and I read, write, and meditate every day.

I’ve already inspired people in my real life.
Now, through these videos, I want to inspire even more.”

## 3. What if no one watches?

“I’ll still keep going.
I can’t control whether others like me or not, but I can control my intention.
And as long as my intention is to inspire others and grow myself, I know I’m aligned.

What matters most to me is growth and value — not money.
I crave growth above all else.

Through blogging, I’ve already grown so much. I’ve learned the power of creation. And the power of documentation.

Now, making videos will challenge me even more — and help me grow even deeper.

and I don’t think about money.
I think about the value I can provide.
 I’ll start small — providing that value every single day.”

## 4. My Pace
“In my first year, I commit to two videos per week — focusing on quantity first, simplicity over perfection.
In my second and third years, I’ll increase to three videos per week.
Because after making 100 videos, the process will become easier, more natural, and more joyful.

And I know — my first 100 videos will probably be terrible.
But that’s okay. I won’t compare, I won’t judge.
I’ll forgive myself and keep moving forward.
Because there’s always a learning curve.”

## 5. My Motivation
“My motivation comes from my dream.
Having dreams at 40 is a wonderful thing — it means I still have curiosity, courage, and the willingness to keep trying.

My dream is to write my awakening book, turn it into a movie, and live in freedom.
And I know a successful YouTube channel will give me the leverage to make all of that possible.

But I also understand the paradox of dreams:
Whether the dream comes true or not doesn’t matter.
What matters is having the dream — and imagining myself living it each morning in meditation.

That alone gives me endless motivation and fuel to keep going.”

## Closing
“This is my clarity.
And clarity is the foundation of success.

In The Art of War, Sun Tzu wrote:
‘The supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without fighting.’

And the greatest enemy on YouTube success is not the algorithm, not competition — it’s yourself.
It’s the fear of uncertainty.
It’s the self-doubt in your ability.
It’s the self-criticism and burnout that come from chasing perfection.
It’s the hesitation of inner conflict.
It’s the distractions that pull you away from your purpose.

But with clarity, the obstacles lose their power, and you win without fighting.

If you’re here with me from the beginning — welcome.
Let’s grow together.

And if you feel inspired, subscribe — and be part of this journey.”
