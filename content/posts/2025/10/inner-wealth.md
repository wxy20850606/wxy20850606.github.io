# Start
In 2022, I obsessed over the market — and lost half of my portfolio.

In 2023, I ignored the market — and gained 60%.

This year, I focused on myself — stayed patient, bought and held —
and gained another 60%.

Warren Buffett once said,

> “The most important quality for an investor is temperament, not intellect.”

In this video, I’ll share how my own losses and gains taught me that temperament — not strategy — is what truly determines your returns.

# Temperament vs Inner Wealth vs Awareness

Temperament is how you emotionally behave under pressure.
It’s what others see — your patience, calm, and consistency when things get difficult.

Inner wealth is your emotional capital — peace, clarity, trust, gratitude, and sufficiency.
It’s what gives temperament its stability.

Awareness is the silent observer — the ability to see your thoughts, emotions, and impulses without being ruled by them.
It’s the root of all transformation.

Awareness is the root, inner wealth is the soil, and temperament is the fruit.

So if you want to build true temperament,
you must first cultivate awareness and grow inner wealth —
because behavior changes only when consciousness deepens.

# Inner Assets and Market Reflections
The market mirrors your state of being.

When my mind was chaotic, my portfolio reflected that chaos — it dropped 50%.
I chased high-growth stocks without clarity, became overconfident, and invested 100%.
When prices fell, I avoided cutting losses — and ended up losing even more.

But when I became patient, centered, and clear, my results followed — with a 60% gain.
Because I could wait for the right price to buy, and hold through both the ups and downs.

So if you want to compound wealth, start from within.
External compounding begins only after internal compounding starts.

1. Patience

I wait for the right price before entering a position — like how I waited a full year to buy LLY.
I hold through market dips without fear — like with AMD. It dropped for a year,
but I kept accumulating. Later, it gained 90%.

Patience turns time into your ally instead of your enemy.

2. Clarity

I stopped chasing every strategy that looked shiny.
I learned that value investing suits me best.

I stay within my circle of competence and invest only in companies with strong moats.

When I lost 52%, I realized I’d need to gain over 100% just to recover —
and that pain taught me humility, not greed.

3. Discipline

I keep 20–30% in short-term bonds — sometimes over 50% when it feels right.
I buy only with a margin of safety and never chase highs.
I avoid emotional trading — buying low, selling high, only when it aligns with logic.


4. Focus

I concentrate my holdings in companies I deeply understand and believe in.
My portfolio is small — fewer than seven holdings.
I ignore daily fluctuations and focus on long-term compounding.

5. Surrender

I no longer set YTD targets or compare my returns with others.
I accept whatever the market gives me.
Surrender doesn’t mean giving up — it means trusting the process
and being at peace with the results.

6. Peace

I stay calm when fear rises and when greed tempts me.
I don’t check my holdings obsessively.
I focus on what brings me peace — reading, writing, walking, creating.

Peace isn’t the absence of volatility;
it’s the ability to stay centered within it.

7. Emotional Sovereignty

The market is the perfect mirror for your emotions.
Every rise reveals attachment; every drop reveals fear.
To grow, I had to separate my self-worth from my portfolio value.

When I stopped letting emotions control me,
I discovered true sovereignty —
inner stability that no market swing could shake.

By building these inner assets,
I not only gained 60% in the market —
I gained awareness.

I began observing myself behind every transaction:
fear during uncertainty,
greed when my portfolio rose,
patience while building a position,
and acceptance when I sold because probabilities changed.

# Awareness Is Behind All Inner Assets

Awareness is the foundation of every inner asset.
Without awareness, patience becomes mere waiting,
clarity becomes rigidity,
and discipline turns into control.

But with awareness — you see yourself clearly.
You know what you’re doing.
You notice the subtle emotions, thoughts, and patterns that drive every decision.
And once you can see them, you stop reacting blindly — you start responding intentionally.

Awareness is the silent observer behind every inner portfolio.
It’s what turns reaction into wisdom, and motion into compounding.

To be a good investor, cultivate your inner portfolio
and practice awareness along the way.
---

# Investing reinfoced my inner porfolio
Once I saw the connection between investing and awareness,
investing stopped being a game of numbers — it became a mirror for my soul.

It’s no longer about outperforming the market.
It’s a spiritual gym.

Every emotion — fear, greed, hope, pride —
is a weight I must learn to lift.

When I panic, I practice awareness.
When I’m tempted by greed, I practice patience.
When I’m uncertain, I practice surrender.
When I feel joy, I practice gratitude.

Each market cycle reflects my consciousness.
Each decision becomes a meditation in disguise —
a moment to act intentionally, not reactively.

Inner wealth compounds just like capital.
The more awareness I build, the less I react.
The less I react, the wiser my actions become.
And wisdom always compounds —
in portfolio, in peace, and in life.

All these realizations brought me back to one simple truth:
what I seek outside already exists inside.

So now, I treat every experience — gains, losses, and emotions —
as training for awareness.
I call it spiritual investing.

---

# Inner Wealth and Awareness Are Universal

Inner wealth doesn’t just improve investing —
it transforms every part of life.

Because awareness is not limited to the market;
it’s the light that reveals truth in everything you do.

When I became more aware, I started making better choices —
not just with money, but with energy.

🧘‍♀️ Health

I used to exercise for appearance.
Now, I train my body to strengthen my mind —
to feel alive, grounded, and centered.

Strength training became a meditation.
Every rep reminds me to stay patient and build inner strength —
the same qualities that guide my investing.

💞 Relationships

In relationships, awareness taught me emotional sovereignty.
I no longer expect others to fill my emptiness.

I became aware that wanting love meant I believed I didn’t have enough.
So instead of seeking love, I chose to become love — to give it freely.

Only then could I stay calm, independent, and whole.

🌱 Personal Growth

In personal growth, awareness helped me shift
from doing more to being more.

I no longer chase outcomes — I align with intention.
Every routine — reading, writing, creating, even managing my portfolio —
has become part of one continuous practice of self-awareness.

The truth is: everything compounds when awareness grows.
Health, wealth, creativity, peace — they all rise together.

Because the same consciousness that builds a strong portfolio
also builds a strong body, a peaceful mind, and a fulfilled heart.

# Closing
After all these years, I’ve realized —
investing was never about numbers or beating others.
It was about observing my own actions and emotions,
and being still instead of reacting.

In the past, I invested for freedom.
Now, I invest from freedom.

From that stillness, clarity arises.
You become a wiser investor, a calmer parent,
a more conscious creator of your own life.

Because the principles that guide great investing —
patience, discipline, and surrender —
are the same principles that create peace, health, and love.

> “The most important investment you can make is in yourself.”
> — Warren Buffett

Now, I no longer measure my returns in percentages.

Because in the end,
the greatest portfolio isn’t made of stocks or bonds —
it’s made of wisdom, awareness, and peace.

And that’s the portfolio I’ll keep compounding
for the rest of my life.

In the next video, I’ll share the habits that help me feel I have enough —
the mindset that keeps me still in chaos.
