# About Me

Deeply inspired by Charlie Munger’s cross-domain approach and Nassim Nicholas Taleb’s "skin in the game" philosophy.

This blog is a version-controlled log of my journey learning and implementing cross-disciplinary frameworks into real-world skin in the game—investing, neural-emotional refactoring, energy management, and heavy barbell training.

Keeping improving.

---

## About This Blog

This blog was originally hosted at `fulltimemammy.com`. 

It documented the beginning of my transformation—the phase where I used English as an isolation sandbox to safely format my pre-installed cultural operating system.

Later, I migrated it to Hugo on GitHub Pages because WordPress plugins for Markdown didn’t fit my needs, using a free domain at the time.

On **June 4, 2025**, I officially moved to my own domain: **everydaydiva.blog**, marking the complete compilation and hardware activation of the **Everyday Diva Operating System**.
